## Daydream Studio LLC
We’re Daydream Studio, a passionate game development group from around the U.S. that works to create games that you've been daydreaming about. On our GitHub page, we will open-source various tools that we use in our development processes for others to use and benefit from.

## Our games
We have made the free game [Cosmic Crusaders](https://daydreamstudio.cc/games/cosmic-crusaders), a fast-paced, retro arcade space shooter where you upgrade your ship to survive scaling waves of enemies inside a dynamic boundary. If you're interested, you can download or play in your browser [right on itch.io](https://daydreamgamedev.itch.io/cosmic-crusaders)!

We’re also currently working on our first flagship title, [Count With Your Hands](https://countwithyourhands.com)! Count With Your Hands is a light-hearted, math-based puzzler that tasks the player with combining numbers with operations and functions to reach a goal number. The game will feature multiple modes, a variety of levels, and a surrealist comedic story. We will share more information about the game as we can on our [Newswire](https://news.daydreamstudio.cc) page.
